Seven tactics have a continuous intensity and are swept at three points; the five discrete tactics are evaluated at one setting. The intensity names a request to the attacker model or a parameter of the transformation; it is not an achieved edit rate measured on the output.

\paragraph{Search settings.}
Beam width $B = 8$, filled as four slots by removal, two by preservation headroom, and two by the family of the final step. Maximum depth $3$. The ordered catalogue at one intensity has $1{,}884$ chains; the intensity grid has $18{,}278$ cells. The search runs the three passes in \S\ref{sec:search} and does not fill that grid. Semantic floor $\tau = 0.80$ with tolerance $\epsilon = 0.02$; headroom skip when predicted preservation falls below $\tau - 0.10$; minimum detectable difference $0.05$ absolute removal rate; order screen of $57$ pairs; search split fraction $0.6$ with seed $20261117$; three stochastic repetitions per document; Holm correction within each depth; per-document $\alpha = 0.05$ and corpus operating point at $1\%$ false-positive rate. Pass~1 uses the swept values in Table~\ref{tab:hyper}. Pass~2 chains each tactic at its operating intensity, the admissible swept level with the highest removal. Pass~3 tries the next stronger swept level on the shortlist and keeps it only when preservation stays at or above $\tau - \epsilon$ and removal rises by at least $0.05$.

\paragraph{Detector settings.}
Scheme parameters are those used at generation: the MarkLLM SynthID configuration recorded in the run manifest, with temperature $0.7$, top-$p$ $0.9$, and $400$ new tokens per document. Watermark depth is 30, implemented as a 30-entry key array (\S\ref{sec:setup}). The scheme's logits processor requires top-$k \le 0$, so decoding uses top-$p$ sampling only. Detection threshold $\alpha = 0.05$ per document, and the corpus operating point at $1\%$ false-positive rate.
