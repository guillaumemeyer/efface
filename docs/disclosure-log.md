## Notice template

> **Subject:** Pre-publication notice — robustness measurement of the MarkLLM
> SynthID implementation
>
> Dear [maintainer],
>
> I am preparing an arXiv preprint that measures how well the MarkLLM SynthID
> reimplementation at commit `0a4fe8c` survives meaning-preserving
> transformations. The study applies twelve tactics and ordered chains of up to
> three tactics to documents generated by `facebook/opt-1.3b` and
> `Qwen/Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct`, and reports removal against the same-config
> detector and preservation under BERTScore, COMET, MAUVE, and length ratio.
>
> The finding is a robustness measurement, not a code vulnerability: I am not
> reporting a key leak, a bypass of the implementation's logic, or a production
> SynthID result. I am writing before posting so that you can correct any
> factual error and tell me if you would like more time to respond.
>
> The draft is available at [link]. I have not attached attack code or the
> generated corpus. If you would like the configuration or the result files, I
> can send them.
>
> Please reply by [date] if you have an objection or a correction. Absent a
> reply, I plan to post on [date].
>
> Regards,
> Guillaume Meyer

## After sending

- Record the date, channel, and message reference in the table.
- Set `Window ends` to ten days after the send date.
- If there is no acknowledgement after five business days, send one follow-up
  and record it.
- If a maintainer asks for more time, record the request and grant up to 90
  days.
- If a factual correction arrives, update the paper and record the change.
